# Draymouth Relining — first-kiln retrospective

The first-kiln retrospective ran ninety minutes with the delivery practice and
the works floor.

What went badly: the castable for the arch arrived eleven days after the
quoted date, which pushed the shutdown window by four days and forced the
utilisation figure to be restated. The crew worked two unplanned weekends to
recover three of those four days.

What went well: the cold-face survey caught the spalling on the south wall
before the scaffold came down, so the repair went in during the same shutdown
rather than needing a second one.

Thessa Oakmoor sat in for the floor handover section and had nothing to add.

Actions agreed: pre-order the second kiln's castable against the shutdown date
rather than the order date, and put the cold-face survey into the standing
shutdown checklist.
