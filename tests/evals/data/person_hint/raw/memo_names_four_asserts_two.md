# Memo — relining programme, purchasing and controller training

Attending the programme review: Thessa Oakmoor, Corwin Vantry, Nerida Selmire,
Ivo Pelloway.

Two decisions from the review.

First, Corwin Vantry now holds signing authority for refractory orders up to
the programme's standing budget line. Orders above it still route through the
works manager. This removes the two-day approval hop that delayed the arch
castable on the first kiln.

Second, Thessa Oakmoor has completed the controller certification and is the
works' first certified operator on the new firing controller. The controller
swap is the relining programme's third phase, so the certification unblocks
commissioning without a vendor engineer on site.

No decision was taken on the sampling schedule or the shutdown calendar; both
carry to the next review.
